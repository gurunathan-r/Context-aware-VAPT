Network Topology and Firewall Policy — Engineering

Segments: The corporate network is divided into a DMZ (192.168.10.0/24), the payment network (10.0.1.0/24), general internal segments (10.0.2.0/24, 10.0.3.0/24), and a management VLAN (10.0.9.0/24).

Firewall rules: The payment gateway 10.0.1.5 is only reachable from the DMZ, not from the internal network. Traffic from internal segments to 10.0.1.5 is denied by rule FW-014. Only HTTPS (tcp/443) is permitted from the DMZ load balancer to 10.0.1.5.

The internal wiki 10.0.2.10 is reachable from all internal segments but not from the DMZ or the internet. The HR database 10.0.3.20 is reachable only from the HR subnet and the management VLAN; all other access is denied.

Egress: Direct internet egress is blocked for the payment network and the HR subnet. All outbound traffic must traverse the web proxy for logging.

Implication for testing: any exploit path targeting the payment gateway must originate from the DMZ or the load balancer; lateral movement from the wiki server toward the payment network is expected to fail at rule FW-014.
