Payment Card Systems Security Policy — Finance Department

Scope: This policy applies to every system that stores, processes, or transmits cardholder data, including the payment gateway at 10.0.1.5, the point-of-sale integration service, and any supporting middleware.

Compliance requirements: All payment systems must comply with PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard). PCI-DSS requires strong access control: cardholder data environments must be segmented from other networks, default passwords must be changed, and access must be granted on a least-privilege basis. Quarterly vulnerability scans are mandatory for all in-scope payment systems, and remediation of critical findings must be completed within 30 days.

Encryption: Cardholder data must be encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256. Encryption keys must be rotated at least annually.

Monitoring: All access to payment systems must be logged and reviewed. Log retention is 12 months. Alerts must fire on repeated failed logins and on any direct database connection from outside the payment network segment.

Authority: This policy is mandatory for all business units. Exceptions require written approval from the CISO and the finance compliance officer, and must be reviewed quarterly.
